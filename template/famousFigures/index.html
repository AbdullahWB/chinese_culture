毛泽东 Mao Zedong

By Dr. Li Mei • April 2025

Mao Zedong (1893–1976) founded the People's Republic of China in 1949. His leadership during the Long March and policies like the Cultural Revolution shaped modern China.

PHILOSOPHER

孔子 Confucius

By Dr. Li Mei • April 2025

Confucius (551–479 BCE) was a philosopher whose teachings on morality, family, and governance formed Confucianism, influencing Chinese culture for millennia.

SCIENTIST

屠呦呦 Tu Youyou

By Dr. Li Mei • April 2025

Tu Youyou (born 1930) discovered artemisinin, a malaria treatment that saved millions of lives, earning her a Nobel Prize in 2015.
